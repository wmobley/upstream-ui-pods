# Managing Campaigns

After creating a campaign, you can manage its details, stations, and publishing state.

## Editing campaign details

1. Open the campaign from the campaigns dashboard.
2. Click the edit icon or **Edit Campaign**.
3. Update any field (name, description, contact info).
4. Save changes.

## Viewing campaign summary

The campaign page displays:

- **Campaign metadata** — name, description, contact information
- **Station list** — each station with its sensor count, measurement count, and status
- **Publication status** — whether the campaign or its stations are published to CKAN

## Campaign permissions

Campaign-level permissions control who can:

- View the campaign and its data
- Edit campaign details
- Upload data to stations
- Publish or unpublish to CKAN

Permissions are managed through the Tapis authentication system. Contact your administrator to adjust access.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search bar on the campaigns dashboard to filter campaigns by name or description. The dashboard shows all campaigns you have access to.
